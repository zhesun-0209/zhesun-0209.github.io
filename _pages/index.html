Zhe Sun

Ph.D. Student at Fudan University

I am a first-year Ph.D. student at the College of Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence, Fudan University, advised by Prof. Yixin Cao. I am also a Student Researcher Intern at Shanghai AI Laboratory. My current research focuses on LLM and agent evaluation. Before joining Fudan, I received my master's degree from Cornell University, where I was advised by Prof. H. Oliver Gao and worked on AI4Science, and my bachelor's degree from Jilin University, where I received the university's highest student honor, the Top Ten Outstanding Student award. I previously interned at Meituan and Alibaba.
