I am a master's student at Cornell University. My interests lie at the intersection of **machine learning**, **renewable energy**, and **transportation systems**.

My recent work focuses on day-ahead photovoltaic power forecasting and reproducible machine-learning benchmarks. I am a co-author of _Toward better integration of solar energy in transportation systems: machine learning benchmarks for day-ahead photovoltaic power forecasting_, published in _Transportation Research Part A: Policy and Practice_ in 2026.

I also maintain [NYSolarForecastLab](https://github.com/zhesun-0209/NYSolarForecastLab), the reference implementation and reproducibility package for this work.
